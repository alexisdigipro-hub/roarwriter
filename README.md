# RoarWriter

Screenwriting in the industry format, in English or Greek. The Mad Lions Lab.

- **Web**: https://alexisdigipro-hub.github.io/roarwriter/
- **Mac and Windows**: the latest release, [Mac (Apple Silicon)](https://github.com/alexisdigipro-hub/roarwriter/releases/latest/download/RoarWriter-Mac-arm64.dmg) · [Mac (Intel)](https://github.com/alexisdigipro-hub/roarwriter/releases/latest/download/RoarWriter-Mac-x64.dmg) · [Windows](https://github.com/alexisdigipro-hub/roarwriter/releases/latest/download/RoarWriter-Windows-Setup.exe). First open on a Mac: right-click > Open, once.

## What it does
- **Formats** (New script asks which; Title page changes it later): Feature Film, TV Drama (one hour, in acts), TV Half-Hour (single camera, cold open), Sitcom (multi-camera: action in capitals, dialogue double-spaced, every scene on a new page), Stage Play (ACT and SCENE centred, names centred, dialogue full width, stage directions in italics), Musical (lyrics), Audio Drama / Podcast (sound and music lines), AV Two-Column (VIDEO | AUDIO, for commercials, music videos, documentaries) and Comic / Graphic Novel (PAGE, PANEL, captions, SFX). Each has its own kinds of line, Enter/Tab, shortcuts (Option/Alt+1… in its order, 0 Unformatted Text), page and PDF; Fountain keeps the format in its title page.
- **Sign in with a RoarMe account** (the same one as RoarMe and Roardino). Scripts are kept on the computer at once and in the account a second later, so they follow you to every device; writing offline works and is sent when the connection is back.
- **The page as the industry lays it out**: Scene Heading, Action, Character, Parenthetical, Dialogue, Transition, Shot, in Courier 12 with the standard margins, on A4 or US Letter.
- **Keys as in Final Draft**: Enter goes on to what usually comes next (heading > action, character > dialogue, dialogue > character), Enter on an empty line goes back to action, Tab / Shift+Tab change the kind, Option+1..9 (Alt+1..9 on Windows) pick one everywhere, in a browser too (Cmd+1..9 also in the Mac/Windows app), 0 Unformatted Text, Shift+Enter a new line in the same element. Typing INT. / EXT. / ΕΣΩΤ. / ΕΞΩΤ. makes a scene heading by itself, and CUT TO: a transition.
- **Suggestions while you type**: the characters' names, INT./EXT. (ΕΣΩΤ./ΕΞΩΤ. in a Greek script), the places already used and the time of day (DAY, NIGHT, ΜΕΡΑ, ΝΥΧΤΑ…). Up/Down to choose, Enter or Tab to take one.
- **Scenes and characters** at the side: the scenes numbered (click to go there; the one you are in is lit), the characters with how many times they speak and in how many scenes.
- **Library**: the script being written first and big (Continue writing, its pages, scenes, time on screen, words), a new Feature Film, TV Drama or Stage Play in one click (Every format for the rest, Import a file), then every script as a small page showing how it begins; All / Mine / Shared, Recent / A–Z and search in the top row.
- **The side and the AI** are as wide as you drag their edge (a double click puts them back), kept per device. Character names can be centred over the dialogue (Settings > Script > Character names); As Final Draft is the default.
- **Zoom**: on a computer − 100% + in the bar, Cmd/Ctrl + and −, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+0 for actual size, or a pinch on the trackpad; on a phone a pinch or ⋯ > Zoom.
- **Pages as they will print**: the script shows the edge between two sheets where each page starts, with its number, as the PDF lays them out (a heading never left at the foot of a page).
- **Title page** on the page itself, as Final Draft lays it out: the title and the episode underlined, Written by and the author, and at the foot the copyright, the draft and the contact (click and type; empty lines do not print). The Title page window also sets the page size.
- **Breakdown**: select a word or phrase and right-click it (on a phone: select, then Tag) to tag it as Cast, Extras, Props, Locations, Wardrobe, Makeup & Hair, Vehicles, Animals, Special Effects, Stunts, Sound & Music or Set Dressing; every place it appears is lit in its colour. The Breakdown button shows it by scene (INT/EXT, location, time, who and what) or by element (with its scenes), and saves it as CSV. Cast and locations come from the script by themselves.
- **Export**: PDF ready to send (title page, numbered pages from 2, no scene heading or name left alone at the foot of a page), **Final Draft** (.fdx), **Fade In** (.fadein) and **Fountain**. **Import** .fdx, .fadein and .fountain from the library. Pasting a screenplay as text turns it into elements.
- **AI** (the AI button, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+A): on the scene with the cursor, Suggest dialogue, Rewrite scene, What comes next, Ideas & notes, or ask anything. The answer is shown first; nothing changes until Insert (after the cursor) or Replace scene.
- **Collaborate** (Share): invite people by email (their RoarMe account) to edit or only to view; the owner changes a role or removes someone, anyone shared with can leave. Several people write in the same script: the others' changes come in within a few seconds, and changes to different lines never overwrite each other. Shared scripts show who owns them in the list.
- **Settings from the list of scripts** (the ⚙ next to the account): what a new script starts with (format, page size, Written by, credit, contact, scene numbers), Writing, Look, Read aloud, the shortcuts and the account.
- **Settings** (the ⚙ in the bar; ⋯ on a phone): Script (format, page size, scene numbers off/left/right/both, (CONT'D), title page), Writing (suggestions, spelling, typewriter scrolling, focus on the scene), Look (white, sepia or midnight page, page edges, zoom), PDF (title page, header, watermark), Read aloud (with or without the action, speed) and the shortcuts.
- **Scene numbers** and **(CONT'D)** on the page and in the PDF (a sitcom's scenes lettered A, B, C).
- **Find & replace** (Cmd/Ctrl+F, Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+F), case and whole words, Greek accents ignored; **rename a character** from the Characters list.
- **Notes on lines**: right-click a selection or Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+M; a yellow mark in the margin, the Notes tab at the side, resolve or delete; shared with the people the script is shared with.
- **Cards**: Page | Cards in the bar, every scene as an index card with what happens, a colour and its length in eighths; drag (or the arrows) to move scenes.
- **Read aloud**: a table read with the device's voices, one per character, the line being read lit.
- **Report** (Breakdown > Report): pages, minutes, scenes, INT/EXT, day and night, locations, who speaks how much.
- **Sides** (Export > Sides): chosen scenes, or every scene of a character, as their own PDF, numbered as in the script.
- **Bold, italic, underline** inside a line (B I U in the bar, Cmd/Ctrl+B, I, U); kept in the PDF, Fountain (**, *, _) and Final Draft.
- **Dual dialogue**: Cmd/Ctrl+D on the second character (or the kind menu > Dual dialogue): the two speak side by side, on the page, in the PDF, Fountain (^) and Final Draft.
- **Revisions** (Settings > Revisions, or the colour chip in the bar): what changes is marked with an asterisk in the revision's colour (Blue, Pink, Yellow, Green, Goldenrod…), scene numbers can be locked (12A, 12B), the PDF names the revision on the pages it changed, can colour them or give only them; next colour, clear the marks.
- **Versions** (the clock in the bar): save a named version, one is kept on its own every day you write; compare any version with now or two with each other (added, changed words, taken out), go back to one (what is there now is kept first).
- **Alternates of a scene** (AI > Scene > Alternates): keep this version of a scene, let the AI write another way it could go, compare and use one.
- **The AI on the whole script** (AI > Whole script): ask anything (the scenes it names, like [12], take you there), Coverage, Continuity (also names written two ways, found at once), Budget pass (what makes it expensive and a cheaper way to the same story value), Pitch package (logline, synopses, treatment, characters, director's statement, comparables; English or Greek), Translate (a new copy in the other language, format and names kept), Runtime & tension. Every report is kept in the script, with copy, .txt and PDF.
- **Research** (AI > Research): facts for the scene from the web with their sources, kept as a note on the scene.
- **Runtime and tension** (Breakdown > Report): the running time from the words, truer every time Read aloud times the speeches, and the AI's tension of every scene drawn along the minutes.
- **Beats** (Page | Cards | Beats | Board): Save the Cat, three acts, Hero's Journey, TV hour or your own; every beat with the page where it should fall, the scene where it does (yours or the AI's) and how far off it is.
- **Storyboard** (Board): frames for every scene, with a picture (click, drop or paste), size, angle, move and what we see; the AI breaks a scene into shots; Shot list (PDF, CSV) and a storyboard PDF.
- **Shooting schedule** (Breakdown > Schedule, or ⋯): strips in the industry's colours with cast numbers and eighths, dragged onto days (or a first plan by location), Day out of Days, PDF and CSV.
- **Dictation** (the microphone): speak in Greek or English, insert it as it is or let the AI lay it out as script (in the browser; in the Mac app the Mac's own Dictation, Fn twice).
- **Writers together**: who else is in the script and in which scene, live; give scenes to writers (Cards > Writer).
- **TV series** (Scripts > A series): the show's bible (logline, the world, characters and places from every episode, what is known since which episode) and its episodes; in an episode, the Series tab at the side.
- **On a phone or tablet** (the web version): the page fills the screen in letters big enough to write in (the PDF keeps the real page), a bar on the keyboard with the seven kinds and Undo / Redo (there is no Tab there), Scenes and characters and the AI over the whole screen, one menu (⋯) for the zoom, the title page and the exports; two fingers on the page zoom it (the lines wrap again), the scripts as a list. Add it to the home screen (Share > Add to Home Screen) and it opens like an app.
- **The Mac/Windows app** adds a real menu (File: Title Page, Export PDF, Save as Final Draft / Fountain; Format: the seven kinds with their shortcuts), Greek and English spelling, and updates itself from new releases.

## Set up (once)
1. Supabase (RoarMe's project) > SQL Editor: run `supabase/roarwriter.sql`.
2. Supabase > Edge Functions: deploy `supabase/functions/roarwriter-ai`; in Edge Functions > Secrets add `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.
